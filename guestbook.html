⚙️ Connect Your Google Form & Sheet (Private Email + Troll-Proof Setup)

To see everyone's verified email privately (so you can spot trolls) while keeping emails 100% hidden from the public website:

  1. Create a Google Form with 3 questions:
    • Name / Handle (Short answer, Required)
    • Favorite RPG or Mod (Short answer, Optional)
    • Message (Paragraph, Required)
  2. In your Form's Settings → Responses, set "Collect email addresses" to Verified. (Now visitors must log into Google, and Google logs their real email in Column B).
  3. In the Form's Responses tab, click Link to Sheets. Keep the Sheet Restricted (Private to Only You).
  4. Inside that Sheet, click the + button at the bottom-left to add a second tab named Public, and paste this formula into cell A1 of the Public tab:
    =QUERY('Form Responses 1'!A:F, "SELECT A, C, D, E WHERE F IS NULL", 1)
    (This copies Timestamp, Name, RPG, and Message—completely skipping Column B (Email)—and if anyone trolls, you just type an x in Column F of your main tab to instantly hide their post!)
  5. Go to File → Share → Publish to web, change Entire Document to Public (only that tab), select Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish, and copy the link.
  6. Paste your Form link and Published CSV link into GUESTBOOK_CONFIG in guestbook.html!
