Recent Updates: BepInEx Conversions & Save Editor v2.1

A quick heads-up on my recent Suikoden I & II HD Remaster updates: Project Kyaro (PKCore) v2026-11-27 hotfixes & Save General Kiba expansion, Kraken Patch, Open Paths Mod, and True Matilda Glitch Restoration converted into BepInEx plugins, plus the Bright Shield Save Editor native Windows desktop build.

Project Kyaro (PKCore) — 2026-11-27 Major Update

Major Release

Stand-alone PKBattlePatch, PKButtonPrompts, and PKReplayPlus DLLs are now consolidated directly into PKCore.dll (delete your existing PKCore folder before installing). Highlights include:

  • Save General Kiba & L'Renouille War Unit (S2): With all 108 Stars recruited (EnableSaveKiba), prevents Kiba's scripted death at Mercenary Fortress Ruins, restores him to Dunan Castle HQ with new dialogue and epilogue card, and deploys him as a standalone general unit in War Battle 16.
  • Bright Shield Rune Enhancement (S2): Great Blessing now also applies Clay Guardian (1.5x DEF), Shining Light adds 2x recruit count to magic damage, and Battle Oath adds an independent 50% Awakened roll alongside Anger.
  • Cook-Off Live Score Helper, Infinite Loop & Accuracy Fixes (S2): Live judge ratings (/20) and top-5 menu recommendations, infinite 12-chef circuit loop with cooldown control, Tetsu flag fix, plus fixed dish score mismatches (Sunomono & Flaming Sea) and active judge taste detection.
  • Clive Quest Replay, Final Boss Gauntlet & Epilogue Restoration (S2): Replayable Clive pursuit questline, configurable L'Renouille Final Boss Gauntlet replay, repeatable Guardian Deity statue rewards, and restored Leona Tavern & Castle Minigames in the epilogue (restricted to post-Beast Rune to prevent earlier story crashes).
  • Battle & Combat Overhaul (S1 & S2): Configurable Battle Camera Zoom-Out (125% default), live Enemy Level & HP in target selection, Memory Auto-Battle, Smart After-Battle Healing, streamlined reusable Command Runes, and Freed Y / Chuchara Medium-range overrides.
  • Performance, Display & Stability: Persistent disk caching for Save Slot party portraits (eliminating scroll stutter), true Win32 Floating Borderless Window Mode, runtime mouse cursor toggle, and a mid-battle Soft Reset crash fix.

Bright Shield Save Editor v2.1.0

The Suikoden II Save Editor is now Bright Shield Save Editor v2.1.0 — a fast, native Windows desktop app (BrightShield.exe) replacing browser overhead with instant tab switching and low memory usage (with the Web and Tauri v1.4.14 editions still available).

  • Auto-Scanned Saves Tab: Automatically detects saves with character portraits, search, and sorting.
  • Backups & Snapshots: Immutable backups\*.bak files, a Restore Diff Modal, and full .zip backup snapshots.
  • 9 Languages & Bundled Fonts: EN, JA, DE, ES, FR, IT, and 简/繁中文 with bundled Noto Sans CJK & icons.
  • 6 Base Themes & 6 Accent Themes: Includes the new Dark Red base theme.
  • Expanded Save Tools: Tessai's Hammers, S1 Transfer Card, location hints, and character Copy/Paste.
