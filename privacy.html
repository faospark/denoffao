Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Faospark's Den of Mods (denoffao.me) handles cookies, local browser storage, analytics, and third-party services in alignment with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), as well as international privacy standards such as the EU GDPR.

1. Static Site, Scope & Personal Information

denoffao.me is a personal static website operated from the Philippines for sharing classic RPG mods, documentation, and blog archives. We do not run user accounts, registration forms, or private databases, we do not collect Sensitive Personal Information (SPI), and we do not sell or trade visitor data.

2. Local Browser Storage

To remember your visual preferences on your own device without sending data to a server, this site stores lightweight keys in your browser's localStorage:

  • Theme preference (denoffao_theme) — remembers your selected RPG window color theme.
  • Language preference — remembers your chosen Google Translate language if used.
  • Cookie consent choice (den_cookie_consent) — remembers whether you chose Accept All or Essential Only.
  • Retro hit counter — stores a local visit increment for the decorative odometer widget in the footer.

3. Google Analytics & Advertising Cookies

When you consent via the Cookie Preferences banner, this site uses Google Analytics (GA4) with IP anonymization enabled to measure aggregate page views and mod documentation traffic.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled on this site, Google and third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites.

4. Third-Party Services & Cross-Border Data Transfers

Static site hosting (GitHub Pages), optional analytics/advertising (Google), mod downloads, discussions, guestbook comments, and optional donations are provided by external platforms—including Nexus Mods, GitHub, Discord, Bluesky, YouTube, PayPal, and Ko-fi. Technical data processed by these services (such as IP addresses or cookies when you visit or interact with them) may be stored on servers located outside the Philippines and is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Your Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act (R.A. 10173)

Under Section 16 of Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), you have the following rights as a data subject:

  • Right to be Informed: To know whether and how your technical or cookie data is being processed, as disclosed on this page.
  • Right to Object & Withdraw Consent: To decline or revoke consent for non-essential analytics or advertising cookies at any time via Cookie Preferences or your browser settings.
  • Right to Access, Rectification & Erasure/Blocking: Because this site stores no user database, all site preferences live in your own browser's localStorage and can be viewed or erased by you at any time by clearing site data.
  • Right to Data Portability & Redress: If you believe your data privacy rights have been violated, you may contact us directly below or lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.
